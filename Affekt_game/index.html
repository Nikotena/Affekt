AFFEKT TOWN

A town where every character has a mood and a memory.
What you say changes how they treat you.

  • Tap the ground to walk, tap a villager to talk
  • Tap a quick phrase or type anything in plain English, then Send
  • WASD or arrows to walk, E next to someone to talk (or click the ground and the villagers)
  • Type anything in plain English and press Enter
  • Try being kind, then rude. Watch the panel: mood, stress and memory change
  • ✕ leaves the conversation
  • Esc leaves the conversation, M toggles music

Replies are written by a language model. Facts like prices come from the game engine.
